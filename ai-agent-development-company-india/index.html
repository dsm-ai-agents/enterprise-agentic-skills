What we build

  • AI sales agents for lead qualification, follow-up, and pipeline management

How We Deliver

1. Discovery & Use-Case Selection

We identify one high-value workflow, define measurable success criteria, and document dependencies before writing any code.

2. Proof of Concept

A bounded pilot with your data, your systems, and your team. Validate the approach with real outputs in weeks, not months.

3. Production Deployment

Security hardening, access control, monitoring, audit logging, and human-in-the-loop review gates before go-live.

4. Maintenance & Optimization

Agent performance evaluation, prompt refinement, guardrail tuning, and recurring governance reviews.
