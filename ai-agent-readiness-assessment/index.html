AI Agent Readiness Assessment

In 30 minutes, a senior practitioner will work with you to identify one high-value workflow, uncover security and data risks, and define the smallest AI-agent pilot worth implementing. No pitch, no generic demo — a structured session that produces a forwardable scope document.

What you get

  • One prioritised workflow: The highest-ROI process to automate or augment with AI agents
  • Readiness diagnosis: Data availability, system access, team readiness, and dependency map
  • Security and risk scan: Data sensitivity, access control gaps, compliance considerations
  • Smallest viable pilot: Scope, timeline, deliverables, and success criteria — sized to get approved
  • Forwardable document: A one-page summary your sponsor can review and forward internally

Who This Is For

  • L&D, HR, or transformation leaders who need a credible first AI agent intervention
  • Operations and IT heads evaluating AI agent adoption for specific workflows
  • Business-unit sponsors who want a scoped pilot, not an open-ended consulting engagement
  • Organisations concerned about AI agent security and governance before deployment
  • Teams stuck between “we should use AI” and “we don’t know where to start”

After the assessment

You will have a clear decision: proceed with a bounded pilot, invest in team enablement first, or address readiness gaps before automation. No obligation, no pressure — just clarity.
