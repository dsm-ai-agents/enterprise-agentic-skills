About Enterprise Agentic Skills

Enterprise Agentic Skills helps Indian organisations move from AI awareness to AI adoption. We identify high-value workflows, build secure AI agents using Microsoft Copilot, Claude, and the right automation stack, train teams, and measure business outcomes.

We are not a large consulting firm that sells a partner and staffs the work with junior resources. Every engagement is diagnosed, designed, and delivered by the people named here — two senior practitioners with complementary expertise across AI agent architecture, enterprise consulting, corporate training, and governed implementation.
